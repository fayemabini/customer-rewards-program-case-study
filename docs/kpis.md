# KPIs & Measurement

## Adoption

- Percentage of eligible customers participating
- Percentage of active customers earning points
- Percentage of customers redeeming points

## Engagement

- Number of qualifying actions per active user
- Course / activity completion tied to reward actions
- Repeat visits or account activity

## Commercial

- Repeat purchase rate
- Revenue from customers participating in the program
- Average order value of participating customers
- Discount / reward cost as a percentage of revenue

## Program health

- Earn-to-redeem ratio
- Points liability / outstanding balance
- Redemption rate
- Expired points
- Fraud / abuse incidents
- Support tickets related to the program

## Operational

- Defect rate after launch
- Number of manual point corrections
- Time to resolve rewards-related support issues

## How I would use the metrics

The goal is not just to report numbers.

For example:

- **High earning + low redemption** may mean rewards are not attractive or redemption rules are confusing.
- **High redemption + weak repeat purchase** may indicate the program is functioning mainly as a discount mechanism.
- **Low participation** may indicate poor visibility or unclear value.
- **High support volume** may indicate program complexity or technical issues.

Metrics should lead to an action or recommendation.
